Why I Play Briar

Briar represents my most aggressive and exciting playstyle. What makes her truly special to me is the way she demands total comitment, when u go in u stay in. Her snobal potential is immense. Once you get a lead, you can constantly invade the enemy jungle and extend your lead. The chaotic, all-in nature of her kit turns every skirmish into a high stakes duel.
