Master Yi | The Wuju Bladesman

A fast-paecd hypercarry capable of taking on the entire team (1v9)

Why I Play Master Yi

Master Yi is the face of the 1v9 carry fantasy. When the game reaches its later stages, his scaling potential is virtually unmatched. I enjoy his fast farming playstyle, allowing me to accumulate resources rapidly while the enemy team wastes time. In chaotic teamfights, his ability to cleanly wipe out multiple targets one after another through perfectly timed Alpha Strikes is one of the most satisfying experiences in League of Legends.
