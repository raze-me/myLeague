Xin Zhao | Seneschal of Demacia

A resilient diver who dictates the early game and secures vital objectives.

Why I Play Xin Zhao

Xin Zhao is my go-to champion for establishing early dominance. His incredibly strong early game allows me to secure First Blood and take control of the map right from the start. I love his reliable engage; when the team needs someone to start the fight, Xin Zhao is always ready. Furthermore, his ability to contest and secure neutral objectives like Dragons and Rift Heralds makes him a cornerstone of any winning composition.

Ability Showcase

P

Determination

Every third attack deals bonus damage and heals Xin Zhao.

Q

Three Talon Strike

The next 3 attacks deal bonus damage and third attack knocks up the enemy.

W

Wind Becomes Lightning

Slashes in an arc then thrusts his spear, slowing and marking targets.

E

Audacious Charge

Charges to an enemy, slowing them and granting bonus attack speed.

R

Crescent Guard

Sweeps his spear to deal damage, knock back non-challenged targets, and block outside attacks.

Strategy Timeline

Early Game

I focus on aggressive level 3 ganks. Contest the scuttle crab and look for skirmishes to establish an early lead for the laners.

Mid Game

Prioritize controlling dragons and the rift herald. Look for picks using W and force team fights early.

Late Game

Transition to a frontline peeler for my ADC and primary engage. And use R when under heavy attack.
